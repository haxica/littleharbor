Play in your browser

Little Harbor runs right in your web browser too, no download needed: play Little Harbor online. It works best on a computer or a tablet. Your game is saved in that browser.

Get in touch

Found a bug, have an idea, or need help? Please open an issue on GitHub and we'll get back to you. Telling us your device and what happened helps a lot.

Questions and answers

How do I play?

Tap a boat on the harbour to go aboard. Drag furniture, decor, food and people out of the tray at the bottom, and tap things to see what they do. Tap the shops and the boatyard to go in, and a house with a coin sign to buy it. Tap "Set Sail!" aboard a boat to head out to sea and go fishing.

How do I visit the other ports?

Just sail there. You don't need to dock: as you sail past a port you can tap its shop, its houses and the people on the quay right from your boat. The sea chart (the map button at the top) takes you straight to any port.

How do I get to the islands?

The Ocean Dream, the big cruise ship, is moored on the left of your own harbour. Swipe over to it, tap the globe above it and pick Coral Bay. The first time, the islands cost 2000 coins (in-game coins, earned by fishing): say yes on the cruise menu. After that it's free to cruise there whenever you like. On the islands you sail the island boats moored in Coral Bay, and from there you can cruise straight home again.

How do I get to the Candy Coast, Spooky Shores and the Neon Coast?

They're the Ocean Dream's other destinations. In the iPhone and iPad app (from version 1.5) each is a one-time purchase: tap it on the cruise menu, then "Unlock". A grown-up answers a quick sum first, then the App Store asks to confirm. Once bought, it's open for good, on every profile. If you opened one with coins in an earlier version, it stays open. In the browser game they cost 4000 in-game coins each. On the Candy Coast you arrive in Gumdrop Bay, with its own boats to sail to Lollipop Lagoon, Sundae Shores and Cocoa Cove. On Spooky Shores you arrive in Pumpkin Harbour, with boats to sail to Skeleton Isle, Ghost Island and Zombie Island. On the Neon Coast (new in 1.4) you arrive in Neon Harbour, with boats to sail to Chrome Isle, Laser Lagoon and Solar Isle.

What are the quests?

Some people have a "!" over their head: tap them to hear their story, and say you'll help. Catch a giant squid, a school of piranhas, a giant lava fish, a huge sea snake, the Frost Pike, the gummy sharks, Big Scoop, the glow-bone fish, Big Chomp, the Glitch Fish or the nano fish; scoop up twinkle fish for a Christmas tree or trash out of the sea; or dive for golden cookies, a ghost's lanterns or a robot's power cells. The "!" is yellow while there's something new to do, and grey while you're on a quest or once you've done it. A quest pays coins the first time; after that you can play it again just for fun.

A sea monster has my boat!

Between some of the islands a friendly sea monster lurks: the Kraken on Spooky Shores, the Gloop on the Candy Coast and the Megabot, a giant robot, on the Neon Coast. When it holds your boat, tap each arm (or strand of candy goo) three times, quickly, and it lets go. Free the boat the first time and you win a little toy of it for your boat.

Can I play in another language?

Yes: Little Harbor speaks English, German and Norwegian. Open the settings (the cog, top right), tap Language and pick a flag.

How do I stop a quest?

Tap the person who gave it to you and choose "Give up". Heading home stops it too. You can always start it again later.

How do I earn coins?

Go fishing and sell your catch at the fish market, or at the shops in the other ports. Coins buy fishing gear, souvenirs, pets and houses.

Is my game saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Can more than one person play?

Yes: tap the cog at the top of the screen and choose a profile. Each profile is its own game, with its own name, boats and coins.

How do I start over?

Tap the cog at the top of the screen, choose "Profile" and tap "Reset" on the profile you want to start over. The game asks you to confirm, since this removes all that profile's boats, friends and coins. The other profiles are kept.

Can I turn off the music or the sounds?

Yes: tap the cog at the top of the screen to switch the music, the sounds, and the waves and engine on or off.

Does it cost anything?

The app is free to download and has no ads or subscriptions. Everything else is earned by playing, with in-game coins. The only purchases are the Candy Coast, Spooky Shores and the Neon Coast, which can each be bought once. A grown-ups' check comes first, so little ones can't buy anything by mistake.

I bought a continent, but it's locked on my new iPhone or iPad

Tap the continent on the cruise menu, then "Restore purchases" next to "Unlock". Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Little Tracks

Little Tracks runs in your web browser too: play Little Tracks online. Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub and say it's about Little Tracks.

How do I play?

Drag track pieces from the tray at the bottom onto the mat; they snap together. Tap a piece to turn it, and tap a switch's red knob to change its way. Drag an engine and some wagons onto the track and off they go. Tap a train to stop it, and tap it again to turn it round. Drag anything onto the tray to put it away. Hold the right mouse button (or use two fingers) to turn and tilt the view.

My train stopped. Why?

Trains stop at the end of the track, behind another train, at a red signal (tap it to make it green) and for a moment at stations. At the end of the track, tap the train twice to turn it round, or lay more track.

How do the cranes work?

Stop a train with container wagons under the cranes and they go to work. In the Harbour the cranes load the ship; when it's full it sails away and a new one comes in. Lorries bring more containers to the pile on the other side: lay track to the siding there and the crane fills your empty wagons. In Freight, fill the wagons at the container pile in the corner, then take them to the logistics centre, where the cranes lift them onto lorries.

Is my game saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. You can keep up to five games; tap the person button at the top to go back to them, or to start a new one.

Does it cost anything?

The app is free to download, with no ads or subscriptions. The Meadow and Mountains maps are free. Harbour and Freight can each be bought once in the iPhone and iPad app. A grown-ups' check comes first, so little ones can't buy anything by mistake.

I bought a map, but it's locked on my new iPhone or iPad

Tap "New game", then "Restore purchases" at the bottom. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.
