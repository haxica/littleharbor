Play in your browser

Little Harbor runs right in your web browser too, no download needed: play Little Harbor online. It works best on a computer or a tablet. Your game is saved in that browser.

Get in touch

Found a bug, have an idea, or need help? Please open an issue on GitHub and we'll get back to you. Telling us your device and what happened helps a lot.

Questions and answers

How do I play?

Tap a boat on the harbour to go aboard. Drag furniture, decor, food and people out of the tray at the bottom, and tap things to see what they do. Tap the shops and the boatyard to go in, and a house with a coin sign to buy it. Tap "Set Sail!" aboard a boat to head out to sea and go fishing.

How do I visit the other ports?

Just sail there. You don't need to dock: as you sail past a port you can tap its shop, its houses and the people on the quay right from your boat. The sea chart (the map button at the top) takes you straight to any port.

How do I get to the islands?

The Ocean Dream, the big cruise ship, is moored on the left of your own harbour. Swipe over to it, tap the globe above it and pick Coral Bay. The first time, the islands cost 2000 coins (in-game coins, earned by fishing): say yes on the cruise menu. After that it's free to cruise there whenever you like. On the islands you sail the island boats moored in Coral Bay, and from there you can cruise straight home again.

How do I get to the Candy Coast and Spooky Shores?

They're the Ocean Dream's other destinations, opened the same way, for 4000 in-game coins each the first time. On the Candy Coast you arrive in Gumdrop Bay, with its own boats to sail to Lollipop Lagoon, Sundae Shores and Cocoa Cove. On Spooky Shores you arrive in Pumpkin Harbour, with boats to sail to Skeleton Isle, Ghost Island and Zombie Island.

What are the quests?

Some people have a "!" over their head: tap them to hear their story, and say you'll help. Catch a giant squid, a school of piranhas, a giant lava fish, a huge sea snake, the Frost Pike, the gummy sharks, Big Scoop, the glow-bone fish or Big Chomp; scoop up twinkle fish for a Christmas tree or trash out of the sea; or dive for golden cookies or a ghost's lanterns. The "!" is yellow while there's something new to do, and grey while you're on a quest or once you've done it. A quest pays coins the first time; after that you can play it again just for fun.

A sea monster has my boat!

Between some of the islands a friendly sea monster lurks: the Kraken on Spooky Shores and the Gloop on the Candy Coast. When it holds your boat, tap each arm (or strand of candy goo) three times, quickly, and it lets go. Free the boat the first time and you win a little toy of it for your boat.

Can I play in another language?

Yes: Little Harbor speaks English, German and Norwegian. Open the settings (the cog, top right), tap Language and pick a flag.

How do I stop a quest?

Tap the person who gave it to you and choose "Give up". Heading home stops it too. You can always start it again later.

How do I earn coins?

Go fishing and sell your catch at the fish market, or at the shops in the other ports. Coins buy fishing gear, souvenirs, pets and houses.

Is my game saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Can more than one person play?

Yes: tap the cog at the top of the screen and choose a profile. Each profile is its own game, with its own name, boats and coins.

How do I start over?

Tap the cog at the top of the screen, choose "Profile" and tap "Reset" on the profile you want to start over. The game asks you to confirm, since this removes all that profile's boats, friends and coins. The other profiles are kept.

Can I turn off the music or the sounds?

Yes: tap the cog at the top of the screen to switch the music, the sounds, and the waves and engine on or off.

Does it cost anything?

There are no ads and no in-app purchases. Everything in the game is earned by playing.
