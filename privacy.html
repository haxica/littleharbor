Privacy policy for Little Harbor and Little Tracks

Home Little Tracks Support Privacy

This policy covers both of our games, Little Harbor and Little Tracks, on iPhone, iPad and in the browser.

The short version

Neither game collects, stores, shares or sells any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Information we collect

None. Neither game asks for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not use your device's camera or microphone.

Your game progress

To remember your game (in Little Harbor your coins, boats, houses, crew and pets; in Little Tracks your track layouts), each game saves its progress on your own device only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or deleting the game inside it, removes it.

In-app purchases

In the iPhone and iPad apps, some extras can be bought once each: in Little Harbor the Candy Coast, Spooky Shores, the Neon Coast and the Funfair Coast, and in Little Tracks the Harbour and Freight maps. Purchases are made and handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's own privacy policy. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details: the apps only ask the App Store which extras have been bought, so they can open them.

Third parties

Apart from Apple's App Store for purchases, the games contain no third-party services: no advertising, analytics, social media or tracking tools, and does not link to other apps or websites from within the game.

Children

Both games are made to be enjoyed by players of all ages, including children. Because they collect no personal information from anyone, they collect none from children either, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Please get in touch through the support page.
