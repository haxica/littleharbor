The short version

Little Harbor does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking.

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Information we collect

None. Little Harbor does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not use your device's camera or microphone.

Your game progress

To remember your game (your coins, boats, houses, crew and pets), Little Harbor saves its progress on your own device only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or choosing "Start over" in the game's settings, removes it.

Third parties

Little Harbor contains no third-party advertising, analytics, social media or tracking tools, and does not link to other apps or websites from within the game.

Children

Little Harbor is made to be enjoyed by players of all ages, including children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Please get in touch through the support page.
